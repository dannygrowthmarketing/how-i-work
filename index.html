Questions owners ask me

Straight answers.

How soon will I see results?

Listing, site and booking fixes show up in calls and enquiries within weeks. Ad data needs about 30 days to judge fairly. Revenue trends need 60–90 days and your billing data to read properly.

What budget do I need for ads?

Less than you think, and only after tracking works. We start with a fixed test budget you set, measure cost per booking, then scale what pays back. Spending before the site and listing are fixed wastes money.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most local sites need a fixed robots setting, clear prices, one phone number, real photos and a booking button. I rebuild only when the old site can't do those.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Google Ads, Meta, Analytics and your Business Profile stay in your name. If we stop working together, you keep every campaign and every number.

Can you handle multiple branches?

Yes. I ran 16 salon locations at once: one profile, one number and one campaign per branch, and a monthly scorecard so you can see which branch is winning and why.

How do you report?

One monthly page in plain language: bookings, bills and revenue by channel, what changed, and what we do next. Matched against your POS or CRM, not just ad dashboards.

What do you need from me?

Admin access to your site, Google profile and ad accounts; a monthly billing summary (new vs returning clients, average bill); and permission to photograph the space.

Do you work with my existing team or agency?

Yes. Often the fastest win is fixing tracking and handing your team a clear weekly list. I can run everything, or audit and guide.

How do you charge?

The week-1 audit is a fixed fee. Ongoing work is a monthly retainer agreed after the audit, when we both know the size of the problem. Ad spend is paid by you, directly to Google and Meta.
